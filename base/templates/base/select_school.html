How to identify your school

Your student registration number follows specific patterns that identify your school:

  • School of Science: Starts with CSC, ARG, FDA, etc.
  • School of Business: Starts with BCOM, BBA, BAF, etc.
  • School of Arts: Starts with ART, MUS, LIT, etc.
  • School of Engineering: Starts with ENG, MEC, ELE, etc.

Select the school that matches the prefix of your registration number.
